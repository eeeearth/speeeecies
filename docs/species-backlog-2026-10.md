# October 2026 species contribution batch

300 research requests, covering 31 proposed activity countries across six
continents: 180 birds, 60 mammals, 36 squamates and 24 amphibians. Each continent
has 50 requests. These counts describe this publication batch, including issues
that may subsequently close.

[Browse the open batch on GitHub](https://github.com/eeeearth/speeeecies/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Aspecies-wave-2026-10).
Claim one task before writing and check for a current claim or existing PR.
Read [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) and [the data-quality guide](data-quality.md).
The [expansion plan](contributor-expansion-plan.md) explains the review findings
and instruction changes behind this batch.

## Screening and limits

On 2026-10-22, each candidate resolved to a matching accepted GBIF species and
an active, non-extinct iNaturalist species. The list was checked against
existing records, retained branches, open PRs and issue history by name and
provider identifier. Domestic-only targets and several ambiguous starting
points were replaced during review.

Each species has at least 300 GBIF month-bearing records in its proposed
country, under the query filters recorded in the [machine-readable manifest](species-backlog-2026-10.json):
present human observations, no flagged geospatial issue, and CC0/CC BY licences.
The manifest retains discovery queries and direct species queries with their
observed counts. Counts can change as GBIF updates its index.

This screen supplies research leads. It does not establish native range,
representative monthly sampling, source support for traits, behaviour fit or
suitability for an existing locale. Some countries contain introduced
populations. Specialist records may require a local behaviour program or a
clearly described representation limitation. The issue bodies explain the
checks still required. No species data or live scenes were added by this batch.
