# The United States queue

The [live ecological simulation](https://www.youtube.com/@jt55401/live)
is working through every US state, one place per setting:

- **rural**: a farm, ranch, forest, desert or mountain lot;
- **coastal**: a lot within sight of the ocean, the Gulf or a Great Lake
  (only in the states that have one);
- **suburban**: a house on a lot with a lawn, beds and street trees;
- **urban**: one street or block, with sidewalks, tree pits and a small
  garden.

The order is chosen so that each new place adds something the stream has
not shown yet: first an unseen EPA Level III ecoregion (a public-domain
map of the country's ecological regions), then an unseen setting within
an ecoregion, a new climate class or a new kind of coast. Places are
grouped into waves of about six.

## Taking a place

Waves 5 to 8 are open to contributors. Each place in them gets an open
[`locale-request` issue](https://github.com/jt55401/speeeecies/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Alocale-request)
with a suggested manifest, data pointers and 10 to 12 candidate species.
Claim one there and follow "Contribute a locale" in
[`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md). The maintainers build the earlier waves
themselves. More issues are posted, six at a time, as the open ones are
claimed.

Every place is a representative lot at a public town, district or county
centroid, never a real property. The place names below are the queue's
working names; the issue gives the exact centroid.
