# Animal species request batch 2026-10b

702 research requests, with 702 distinct GBIF species keys and iNaturalist IDs. This batch adds research tasks to the open animal request queue; it does not add finished records or live scenes.

[Browse open requests](https://github.com/eeeearth/speeeecies/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Aspecies-request+label%3Aanimal) and read [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) before claiming one.

## Screening and limits

Each published lead resolved to an accepted GBIF species and a matching active, non-extinct iNaturalist species during screening. It had at least 300 GBIF country records with a month, present human-observation status, no flagged geospatial issue and a CC0/CC BY licence. The linked query and the provider IDs are in the [machine-readable audit](species-backlog-2026-10b.json).

The screen checked 922 GBIF keys and accepted 794 candidate taxa. The audit records rejection counts by reason. Published issue count can be lower than accepted count because screening includes a small overflow.

These checks do not establish native range, representative seasons, anatomy, behaviour or live-scene fit. Contributors must recheck provider records and research those claims. The [later-batch reserve](species-reserve-2026-10.md) is unpublished and has a lower screening status.

5 unsuitable leads were closed during the publish audit ([#522](https://github.com/eeeearth/speeeecies/issues/522), [#567](https://github.com/eeeearth/speeeecies/issues/567), [#711](https://github.com/eeeearth/speeeecies/issues/711), [#757](https://github.com/eeeearth/speeeecies/issues/757), [#993](https://github.com/eeeearth/speeeecies/issues/993)) and excluded from this batch.
