Locales

A locale is a public place the live simulation can show: a city or district centroid, its climate, a generic plot and at least 8 species from the gallery. A locale joins the live rotation after its pull request is merged, CI passes, and the maintainers' import succeeds; see AGENTS.md ("Contribute a locale") to add one. Plants are phase 2, so every locale uses a default plant catalog for its climate for now.
